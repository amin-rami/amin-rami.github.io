Hello! I am Mohammadamin “Amin” Rami, a scientific research intern at [ISTA](https://ista.ac.at/en/home/), where I work in the [Data Science, Machine Learning, and Information Theory](https://ist.ac.at/en/research/mondelli-group/) group under the supervision of [Prof. Marco Mondelli](http://marcomondelli.com/). My research broadly focuses on the mathematical foundations of AI, at the intersection of machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, and information theory.

I received my Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, specializing in machine learning and information theory, from [Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.ip-paris.fr/), where I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Aslan Tchamkerten](https://www.tchamkerten.org/). I received my Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering from [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.ir/).
