Mohammadamin Rami

Hello! I am Mohammadamin “Amin” Rami, a scientific research intern at ISTA, where I work in the Data Science, Machine Learning, and Information Theory group under the supervision of Prof. Marco Mondelli. My research broadly focuses on the mathematical foundations of AI, at the intersection of machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, and information theory.

I received my Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, specializing in machine learning and information theory, from Institut Polytechnique de Paris, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Aslan Tchamkerten. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.

  1. Effective Context in Transformers: An Analysis of Fragmentation and Tokenization
    Amirmehdi Jafari Fesharaki, Mohammadamin Rami, and Aslan Tchamkerten
    May 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    Transformers predict over a representation of a sequence. The same data can be written as bytes, characters, or subword tokens, and these representations may be lossless. Yet, under a fixed context window, they need not expose the same information to the model. This raises a basic question: how does the choice of representation change what a finite-context predictor can achieve?
